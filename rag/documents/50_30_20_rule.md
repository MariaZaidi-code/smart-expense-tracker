# 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule

The 50/30/20 rule is a simple budgeting framework. Allocate 50% of after-tax income to needs
(housing, groceries, utilities, transport), 30% to wants (dining out, entertainment, shopping),
and 20% to savings and debt repayment. Review monthly and adjust if spending drifts.

Source: Personal finance education material.
